0 · Time Desk (read this first)

The rule
Before 2:00 AM on Sun 11/2 the clocks were on PDT = UTC−7.
At 2:00 AM PDT (= 09:00 UTC) they fell back to 1:00 AM, and from then on it was PST = UTC−8.
So every local time from 1:00 to 1:59 AM happened twice: once in PDT (08:00–08:59 UTC) and again in PST (09:00–09:59 UTC).

To convert: UTC → PDT subtract 7 h; UTC → PST subtract 8 h. A local “1:xx” is ambiguous until you know which pass it was.		 Quick table

Clocks come in three kinds. Fill in each device you meet: (a) records in UTC; (b) local time, adjusts itself (phones, network-synced registers); (c) local time, set by hand or never adjusted, so after the change it may read an hour off. Counters that only go up (sequence numbers, transaction numbers) tell you order no matter what any clock says.
