MEMORANDUM

TO:   Detective (you)
FROM: The Willow Bend bake-off committee (fictional)
DATE: Sat, late October   RE: A missing blueberry pie

This is a short practice case. Nothing here relates to the real case you may play later. It uses the same tools, just a made-up story about a pie.

June Abernathy set her prize blueberry pie to cool on her kitchen windowsill at about 2:00 PM. At 3:00 it was gone. The empty tin turned up in Walt Penrose's side yard, by a gap in the fence. June would like to know what happened. Nobody is hurt. There is no crime beyond a missing pie.

Work the file the same way you will in the real game. Typical time is about ten minutes. You get one accusation. — W.B.
How to play (the tools, in miniature)
1. Open Case Files. Read the papers and photos. Pin the useful ones to the Evidence Board.
2. In Tools & Lab, crack June's recipe tin (the code is in a document), send a lab request, and walk the side yard. Some observations can only be logged there, and they become new exhibits.
3. In the Interview room, ask Walt in your own words or present an exhibit. Watch his face when a question makes him uncomfortable. If he admits something important, it is filed as a new statement.
4. Stuck? Hints come in three levels and each one costs points.
5. When you're sure, Accuse: who, why, how, and when, with evidence for each. That's one shot, then a short scored reveal.
