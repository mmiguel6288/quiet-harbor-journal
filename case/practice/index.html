How interviews work. Ask anything in your own words, or hold 🎙 and speak. People answer from what they know, and they may dodge or fib about things that embarrass them. Repeating a question rarely changes a story; showing the right evidence, or pointing out a specific contradiction, does. Watch faces: a look away means something is uncomfortable, not necessarily guilty. Nothing on this page has anything to do with the real case.

The situation. June Abernathy's prize blueberry pie vanished from her windowsill between 2 and 3 PM. The empty tin turned up in Walt's side yard. Find out what happened.
